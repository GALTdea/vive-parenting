whatKidsDo:
  - Children join a family session (using a code from the host) and participate in a fictional market game with invented companies and changing share values.
  - They make choices in the context of the game’s rules and see how those choices play out in the simulation.
  - We verified the public landing and setup flow; we have not independently completed a full multi-player game session.
whyInteresting: Money and markets can feel abstract to young children. ShareSpark frames those topics as a shared family activity—useful as a conversation starter rather than a lesson in real-world investing.
parentNotes: Designed for shared family participation; plan to play together rather than handing a device to a child alone. This is a simplified simulation, not real investing, and we cannot confirm it improves financial literacy outcomes. A family code is used to join a session—treat that code like a private room password and share it only with people you trust.
