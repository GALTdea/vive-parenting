whatKidsDo:
  - Children explore one glass of water and drag a temperature slider to see how heating and cooling change what they observe on screen.
  - They can switch between a glass view and an atoms view to connect everyday water with a particle-level model (features described on the public site; we verified the live interface loads but did not exhaustively test every control).
  - Preset buttons (such as freezer, ice, room temperature, boiling, and steam) offer quick jumps to familiar scenarios; some presets appeared locked behind progression on the page we opened.
whyInteresting: Marble Water Lab turns an abstract science topic into something kids can manipulate directly. Instead of only reading about states of matter, they change temperature and watch representations update in real time.
parentNotes: This is a standalone web experience separate from Marble's broader iPad application—do not assume iPad app policies or features apply here. Visualizations are educational models, not exact physical measurements. We verified the public website and observed core controls; we have not independently completed any quiz or locked content. Preview alongside your child if you want to discuss what the models simplify or omit.
